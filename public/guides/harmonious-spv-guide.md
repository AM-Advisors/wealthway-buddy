## Executive Overview: The Modern SPV Imperative

Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) are the primary mechanism for mobilizing concentrated capital into high-conviction venture and private equity opportunities. Whether executing a follow-on pro-rata allocation, structuring a single-asset syndicate, or acquiring secondary equity, managers require speed, institutional compliance, and a frictionless investor onboarding experience.

Traditional fund administration introduces significant friction into SPV operations:
- **Lengthy Setup Timelines:** Multi-week waits for entity formation, Delaware filings, and commercial bank accounts that risk missing closing deadlines.
- **Manual, Clunky LP Onboarding:** Fragmented PDFs, repeated KYC/AML submissions, and high friction for high-net-worth individuals and institutional co-investors.
- **Fragmented Operations:** Disconnected spreadsheets, escrow accounts, tax preparers, and cap table managers.
- **Tax & Compliance Overhead:** Uncoordinated annual Schedule K-1 issuance and state franchise tax tracking that burden internal teams.

**Harmonious solves this by delivering an end-to-end, technology-first SPV administration platform** combined with dedicated senior fund accounting specialists. Managers launch SPVs in hours, onboard accredited LPs with universal profiles, close deals with clean single-line cap table representation, and receive comprehensive full-lifecycle accounting through asset liquidation.

## Core SPV Services Matrix

### 1. Entity Formation & Structural Setup
- **Delaware Series or Stand-Alone Entities:** Fast creation of Delaware Series LLC/LP or standalone Delaware LLC/LP vehicles optimized for single-asset venture investments.
- **Tailored Legal Documentation:** Standardized operating agreements, subscription agreements, and LPA drafting supporting bespoke management fees, carry, and hurdle terms.
- **Federal EIN & Registered Agent:** Instant EIN acquisition and continuous Delaware registered agent management.
- **Side Letter Administration:** Compliance with bespoke LP terms, MFN clauses, co-investment rights, and reporting covenants.

### 2. Investor Onboarding & Regulatory Compliance
- **Automated KYC/AML & Sanctions Screening:** Instant identity verification, Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) checks, and automated OFAC screening.
- **Regulation D 506(b) & 506(c) Workflows:** Built-in workflows for pre-existing substantive relationships (506b) and automated third-party CPA/attorney accredited investor certification (506c).
- **Universal LP Passport:** Investors complete verification once and seamlessly subscribe to subsequent SPVs or funds with one click.
- **Qualified Purchaser & Institutional Onboarding:** Frictionless verification for entities, family offices, trusts, and foreign investors.

### 3. Dedicated Banking & Treasury Management
- **Dedicated Commercial Bank Account:** Segregated FDIC-insured account opened specifically for the SPV with zero co-mingling of funds.
- **Automated Wire & ACH Reconciliation:** Real-time matching of incoming investor wires against executed commitments.
- **Live Deal-Room Tracking:** GPs track committed capital, signed subscription agreements, and received funds in real time.
- **Secure Closing Escrow & Outbound Funding:** Controlled multi-signature wire release directly to the portfolio company upon closing.

### 4. Cap Table & Closing Execution
- **Clean Single-Line Representation:** The SPV appears as a single unified shareholder on the target company’s capitalization table, preserving simplicity for founders.
- **Digital Instrument Execution:** Seamless signing and counter-execution of SAFEs, Convertible Promissory Notes, Preferred Stock Purchase Agreements, and side letters.
- **Automated Closing Binders:** Comprehensive digital compilation of all signed agreements, banking confirmations, and cap table ledgers delivered to all parties.
- **Ongoing Cap Table Monitoring:** Continuous tracking of ownership percentage, dilution across follow-on financing rounds, and pro-rata rights.

### 5. Fund Accounting, Valuations & Tax Coordination
- **GAAP Fund Accounting:** Complete double-entry general ledger accounting maintained by dedicated private market accountants.
- **ASC 820 Fair-Value Valuations:** Regular valuation marks based on subsequent priced financing rounds, 409A valuations, and liquidity events.
- **Schedule K-1 Preparation & Delivery:** Full tax preparation, electronic federal/state partnership tax filings, and secure 1-click K-1 distribution to LPs via the portal.
- **State Franchise Tax Compliance:** Automated annual Delaware franchise tax filings and registered agent fee management.

### 6. Distributions & Vehicle Dissolution
- **Automated Waterfall Calculations:** Execution of American or European distribution waterfalls, calculating return of capital, preferred return hurdles, and carried interest splits.
- **Flexible Liquidity Execution:** Handling of cash distributions or in-kind public stock distributions directly into investor brokerage accounts.
- **Tax Withholding & Reporting:** Comprehensive calculation of state and federal withholding for domestic and foreign investors.
- **Formal Entity Dissolution:** Orderly wind-down, final tax return filing, Delaware certificate of cancellation, and vehicle dissolution once the asset is realized.
