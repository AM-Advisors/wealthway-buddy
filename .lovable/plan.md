# Harmonious onboarding training videos

## Goal
Create two polished, branded instructional videos using clearly labeled synthetic QA records only:

1. **Investor onboarding** — from invitation through About You, Verification, Sign, and Fund.
2. **New Fund and investor setup** — covering both the fund manager’s request flow and the Harmonious staff steps required to create/configure the Fund, followed by adding investors.

Each guide will be delivered in:
- 16:9 landscape for the portal, presentations, and desktop viewing.
- 9:16 vertical for mobile sharing.
- Professional voiceover with synchronized burned-in captions.
- A separate written script/checklist that can be provided alongside the video.

## Visual and editorial direction
- Recreate the current Harmonious interface as a guided screen walkthrough rather than recording production.
- Use the approved Harmonious identity: navy `#142647`, teal `#5DC6D1`, white, and ink `#221F20`; Rubik headings and Poppins body copy.
- Use a clearly labeled synthetic training Fund, investor, manager, amounts, and documents. No production records, personal information, provider actions, signatures, emails, or money movement.
- Use a calm, precise training style: cursor movement, focused zooms, highlighted controls, short callouts, and steady pacing.
- Keep role boundaries explicit, especially where only Harmonious staff can act.

## Video 1 — Investor onboarding

### Storyboard
1. Open a synthetic invitation and explain signing in with the invited email.
2. Confirm or correct prepared information and choose who is investing: an existing Investment Profile or a new, separate profile.
3. **About You** — enter the investing party’s factual information; reinforce that the login account is not the investing party.
4. **Verification** — complete identity, tax, financial-background, accreditation, and eligibility steps; explain “In review” states.
5. **Sign** — review the exact prepared documents, certify the applicable tax form, and complete signing; explain that provider confirmation controls status.
6. **Fund** — show the wire-fraud warning, identity reconfirmation, secure instructions, and “I’ve Sent My Wire.” Clearly state that this does not mark the investment funded; only reconciled receipt does.
7. Close with resume guidance and the distinction between onboarding complete, admitted, and funded.

### Supporting instructions
Provide a concise investor checklist organized exactly as **About You → Verification → Sign → Fund**, plus short safety notes about email links, sensitive information, signatures, and wire verification.

## Video 2 — Add a new Fund and investors

### Storyboard
1. Explain the two roles and handoff:
   - The fund manager submits the four-step new-Fund request and tracks progress.
   - Harmonious staff creates/configures the canonical Fund and completes staff-controlled setup.
2. Show the manager request flow: Fund type, basic information, offering/setup details, review, and submission.
3. Show the staff Client 360 path: **Funds & SPVs → Add Fund**, including the fact that this creates a closed, empty Fund shell without investors, documents, banking, or integrations.
4. Walk through the single Fund Setup architecture: identity, entity, economics/classes, offering documents, banking, EIN/SS-4, administration/regulatory configuration, investor requirements, and final review—without implying completion of regulated or provider-controlled actions.
5. Enter the manager Fund workspace and open **Investors**.
6. Show the available investor paths: invite, create a new record, prepare an investor draft, bulk add, and—where authorized—add an existing investor.
7. Demonstrate the Onboarding Link area: staff manages link creation/replacement/disablement; exact-Fund managers can view, copy, and preview it.
8. Show Prepared versus Claimed, onboarding/readiness states, and the investor roster without implying access, confirmation, funding, or approval.
9. Close with the role handoff and the rule that emails send only through an explicit send action.

### Supporting instructions
Provide a role-separated checklist with columns for **Fund Manager**, **Harmonious Staff**, and **Investor**, including prerequisites and stop points.

## Production approach
- Build the videos as deterministic motion-graphics walkthroughs so both aspect ratios stay consistent and no live data is exposed.
- Create reusable branded screen, cursor, caption, progress, warning, and role-label components.
- Record a neutral, professional voiceover and time captions to the narration.
- Keep external identity-verification and signing-provider screens schematic unless approved synthetic provider sandboxes are available; never simulate a successful regulated check or signature as a real event.
- Render and inspect key frames before final export, then verify dimensions, duration, audio, and caption legibility for all four MP4s.

## Acceptance checks
- All screens and wording reflect the current application flow.
- Investor sequence remains About You → Verification → Sign → Fund.
- The investing party is always Person → Investment Profile → Investment; the login account is never presented as the investor.
- No action implies an automated filing, tax payment, refund, bank debit, delegated money authority, ACH/wire initiation, provider money movement, verified bank account, funded state, or completed compliance decision.
- Staff-only and manager-available actions are clearly distinguished.
- All names, emails, amounts, Funds, documents, and statuses are synthetic and visibly marked as training examples.
- Captions fit safely in both formats and the story remains understandable with audio muted.
