# Fund & SPV Setup, Formation and Launch

## Outcome
Harmonious employees can find every non-retired Fund and SPV in one Operations listing, create a new one, open it, and edit permitted general setup details. “Fund Setup” is visible under Funds & SPVs in the Operations sidebar. The selected Chapter 1 series SPV has a single guided, evidence-based setup → entity formation → launch view, with current state and missing actions visible to an admin.

## Build
1. Add a Fund Setup sidebar entry and a Funds & SPVs listing action that opens the selected Fund’s canonical setup; offer New Fund/SPV from the listing. Make the listing searchable and clear about closed, retired, duplicate-review and setup states. Retain existing fund records and URLs; do not create another Fund model.
2. Reuse the canonical eight-section Fund Setup and existing server-authorized formation and launch services in one Fund workspace. Show evidence, checklist, outstanding requirements and historical decisions. Remove misleading “coming next phase” text where sections already work. Link to the existing protected banking, EIN, documents and investor workspaces rather than duplicating their records or exposing them broadly.
3. Let every **individual Harmonious staff member** access the Fund/SPV listing and create or edit non-sensitive general setup fields through server-side staff checks. Keep Sales in a commercial-safe view with no investor, bank, tax, KYC, or compliance data. Sensitive configuration, formation evidence confirmation, approvals and launch decisions remain with their existing specialized permissions and second-person checks; a team assignment or sidebar link grants no authority. Add scoped checks to every new read/write endpoint and tests for Sales, operations, managers and outsiders.
4. Use **Chapter 1, a series of Storybook Ventures** with the existing setup record as the walkthrough. First inspect its duplicate-review state and available source documents read-only; show precisely which formation and launch conditions have credible evidence. Save only specific verified details supported by those records and the authorized actor. Never infer a filed/accepted entity, EIN receipt, bank verification, document approval, investor eligibility, launch approval or funded state from the fund name or a placeholder. If evidence or second-person approval is absent, leave the fund pending and show the admin the next action.
5. Exercise the workflow with labeled synthetic QA records where a complete launch is needed, without emailing clients, moving money or creating fictional production states. Run focused authorization/workflow tests and an authenticated browser walk-through of listing → selected fund → setup → formation → launch readiness; report what could and could not be completed on the live fund.

## Technical boundaries
- The selected live setup is tied to the Chapter 1 series SPV record that already has a `fund_setups` row; another identically named Fund record exists. Match by immutable Fund ID, never name, and do not merge or consolidate either.
- Fund setup currently has overlapping older screens; route the new entry to canonical records rather than treating legacy setup rows as a second source of authority. Read existing access facts server-side; any broader general-edit capability must be explicit and scoped, not implemented by making all staff `admin` or bypassing authorization.
- No automated filings, tax payments, refunds, bank debits, ACH/wire initiation or provider money movement. No production record updates without verified evidence and an authorized user; a walkthrough may legitimately stop at “Needs evidence” or “Needs approval.”
